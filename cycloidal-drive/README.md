## Overview

This project focuses on the design and modelling of a cycloidal drive mechanism for robotic actuation. The objective is to explore its mechanical construction, motion transmission, and potential for compact robotic actuators.

A related research direction investigates how wear-induced clearance affects torque transmission and how adaptive preload compensation could help maintain actuator performance.

## Engineering Work

- **CAD Design:** Modelling cycloidal discs and associated mechanical components.
- **Assembly Design:** Developing the drive assembly and studying component interactions.
- **Motion Analysis:** Exploring the mechanism's motion and transmission behaviour.
- **Research Direction:** Investigating clearance, preload, wear, and torque transmission.
