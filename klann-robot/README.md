## Overview

This project involves designing and fabricating a walking mechanism based on the Klann linkage. The mechanism converts rotary input into a walking motion through a coordinated arrangement of mechanical links.

Unlike a conventional legged robot with independently actuated joints, the Klann mechanism generates its gait through its linkage geometry.

## Engineering Work

- **Mechanism Design:** Developing the linkage geometry and component arrangement.
- **CAD Modelling:** Designing the links and mechanical assembly.
- **Fabrication:** Manufacturing and assembling a physical prototype.
- **Motion Analysis:** Examining the relationship between rotary input and foot trajectory.
