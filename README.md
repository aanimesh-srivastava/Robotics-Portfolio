# Aanimesh Srivastava Portfolio

**Mechanical Engineering | Robotics R&D | Mechatronics | Autonomous Systems**

I am a Mechanical Engineering student at VIT Vellore, focused on robotics, autonomous systems, mechanical design, and intelligent control.

My work spans robotic mechanisms, legged locomotion, embedded systems, ROS 2, robot simulation, and reinforcement learning. I am interested in developing robotic systems by combining mechanical engineering with electronics, control, and software.

I founded **Team Artemis**, a student-led robotics team working on platforms including hexapods, quadrupeds, and autonomous rovers. I have also gained research experience with SMA/Nitinol actuators at DRDO.

## Projects

### 1. Cycloidal Drive
**Mechanical Design | CAD | Robotic Actuation**

Exploring cycloidal drive mechanisms for robotic actuators, with an interest in torque transmission, backlash, wear, and clearance compensation.

- Cycloidal mechanism design and CAD modelling.
- Mechanical component and assembly design.
- Investigation of clearance and preload compensation.

### 2. Quadruped Robot
**Legged Robotics | NVIDIA Isaac Sim | Reinforcement Learning**

Developing a quadruped robot model and investigating locomotion, stability, and learning-based control through simulation.

- Robot modelling and simulation.
- Joint behaviour and robot dynamics.
- Reinforcement learning experiments.

### 3. Hexapod Robot
**18-DOF Robotics | Inverse Kinematics | ROS 2**

Designed and developed an 18-degree-of-freedom hexapod platform through Team Artemis, integrating mechanical design with robotic control.

- Three degrees of freedom per leg.
- Inverse kinematics and gait generation.
- ROS 2 joint-control workflows.
- Gazebo simulation and RViz visualisation.

### 4. Bipedal Robot
**Robot Modelling | Kinematics | Simulation**

Developing a bipedal robot model to investigate joint coordination, locomotion, and stability.

- Robot geometry and mechanical modelling.
- URDF-to-USD workflow.
- Joint configuration and simulation.
- Investigation of balance and stability.

### 5. Self-Balancing Robot
**Arduino | MPU6050 | PID Control**

Developed a self-balancing robot integrating inertial sensing, embedded programming, and feedback control.

- MPU6050-based motion sensing.
- PID-based balance control.
- Arduino and L298N motor-driver integration.

### 6. Klann Mechanism Robot
**Linkage Design | Kinematics | Fabrication**

Designed and fabricated a walking mechanism based on the Klann linkage, exploring how mechanical linkages generate walking motion.

- Linkage design and CAD modelling.
- Mechanical assembly and prototype fabrication.
- Investigation of linkage-driven gait.

### 7. Pick-and-Place Robot
**Robotic Manipulation | Motion Planning | Control**

Exploring robotic manipulation through pick-and-place tasks, focusing on coordinated movement and object handling.

- Robot modelling and task setup.
- Motion planning and control.
- Simulation and task evaluation.

### 8. Autonomous Rover
**ROS 2 | Nav2 | SLAM Toolbox | Gazebo**

Worked on an autonomous rover software workflow involving navigation, mapping, and path planning.

- ROS 2 navigation using Nav2.
- Mapping and localisation with SLAM Toolbox.
- A* path planning.
- Gazebo simulation and RViz visualisation.

## Engineering Workflow

1. **Design:** Develop mechanical concepts, CAD assemblies, and engineering drawings.
2. **Model:** Study mechanism geometry, robot kinematics, and dynamics.
3. **Integrate:** Combine actuators, sensors, embedded electronics, and software.
4. **Simulate:** Evaluate robot behaviour in simulation.
5. **Test and Iterate:** Assess results, identify failures, and improve designs.
